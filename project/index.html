The project

Build a generative AI assistant over documents you care about. Ship it to GitHub. Defend the architecture you chose.

How it is scored

100 points · pass at 60 · marked live on Thursday

CriterionPointsWhat full marks looks like

& justification		 25 You picked RAG, an agent, or a hybrid, and can say why in one sentence tied to your use case. Wrong-but-justified beats right-but-unexplained.
Working retrieval 25 Your own documents, sensible chunking, relevant results, and answers that are visibly grounded — with citations to source and page.

The five required components

Tick these off as you go. "Working" means it runs in Colab — no deployment, no frontend, no Docker.

The week

Monday

Pitch. Teams form, ideas go on the board, you choose your document set.

Tuesday

Build. Gate by 2:30 — documents loaded, chunked, embedded, one query returning something. Running, not polished.

Wednesday

Harden + README. Retries, caching, your numbers table. README clinic 2:00–2:20, written in the room.

Thursday

Present. Red team in the morning, four minutes each in the afternoon, scored live.

SDAIA repository requirements

All six are required. Together they are the 15 repository-quality points — the cheapest marks on the rubric.
