Why the number is "approximate"

This page uses a documented heuristic rather than a real tokenizer, because every page on this site has to work with the wifi off: roughly four characters per token for Latin script, roughly two for Arabic, punctuation on its own, and whitespace attached to the token that follows it. A real tokenizer would differ by a few either way. The ratio between the two scripts — the thing that shows up on your bill — is right.
