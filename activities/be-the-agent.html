Activity 3 · Be the Agent

You are the model. Each turn you may call one tool or give a final answer. You do not execute anything — you request, and the runtime executes for you. 6 points for the right answer, −1 per wasted step, +2 for recovering from a tool error.

Steps used 0
Minimum 3
Recovered no
Best —

Your goal

"Find the cheapest flight to Jeddah on Tuesday and tell me the total for 3 passengers."

Answer with the final total in SAR, including taxes.

Tools you may request

search_flights(destination, day)

Returns available flights with the base fare per passenger. Does not include taxes.

get_price(flight_id, passengers)

Returns the full total for that flight including taxes and fees. This is the number the user asked for.

calculate(expression)

Arithmetic. Reliable, unlike doing it in your head — or in a model's head.

final_answer(total_sar)

Stop the loop and answer the user. You only get one of these.
